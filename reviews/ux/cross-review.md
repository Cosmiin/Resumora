## Final strategy questions — inbox 003, 2026-10-23

**UX-F01 / CR-04/07/17: high score with missing authorization.** Accepted. Primary score card uses a persistent amber text warning beside the number, repeated on comparison and export preview. No eligible/ready/likely-hired label. Added an operable synthetic edge scenario:90/100 with qualification0/10 and “current professional authorization in country X” missing; a translated diploma is not equivalent. It demonstrates the guardrail, never the actual parsed job or legal determination. Export is not blocked because a missing credential is information, not our authority to determine eligibility. `prototype/app.js`: criticalGap(), score(), ats(), compare(), preview(), edge-credential action.

**UX-F02 / CR-06/19: exclusive five-status precedence.** Accepted as proposed display mapping, pending D-11 approval. First validate fact confirmation and source; generated text never supplies evidence. (1) Confirmed fact with exact selected document term → included. (2) Confirmed fact with approved equivalent selected in document → verified_equivalent. (3) Confirmed fact with neither selected → verified_omitted. (4) Plausible but unconfirmed fact → awaiting_confirmation, even if suggested wording contains the word. (5) No supported/plausible evidence → unsupported. Each row has one primary status, with separate badges for confirmation basis, RO↔EN equivalence/alias version and required/preferred priority. Exact+equivalent in the same selected document uses included with equivalence badge. Qualifications require appropriate evidence; aliasing cannot establish a licence or country-specific permission. Prototype shows all five fixture states and an RO↔EN badge; it does not implement freeform matching. `research/ux/screen-specifications.md` and integrated PRD carry this rule.

**UX-F03 / CR-19: unknown second title.** Accepted. Title2 remains optional and may be blank. No auto-invention, penalty, onboarding block or inferred historical title. Title1 may also remain unknown during review; missing positioning is a question, not a licence to create a title. Two independent fields are grouped away from employment title in review/editor. `identityFields()` labels title2 optional; save requires name/email, not invented titles.
