# The prepare screen becomes one ring

The owner picked option B ("One ring") on 20 Nov from three mock-ups (https://claude.ai/artifact/KnvyTxdM7Qhor1dnXprFpH). The old screen ("Putting your answers on your CV…", three bouncing dots, "All roles at once." and a list of roles with "Working…") looked untidy and wrapped badly.

## Task 1: One ring while every role is prepared

```
files: apps/web/src/cv/enrich/EnrichPath.tsx, apps/web/src/cv/enrich/enrich-path.test.tsx, apps/web/src/styles/focus.css, apps/web/src/i18n/en-basecv.ts, apps/web/src/i18n/ro-basecv.ts
new_files: apps/web/src/cv/enrich/PrepRing.tsx, apps/web/src/cv/enrich/prep-ring.test.tsx
deps: none
size: ~150 lines
```

**Goal.** While the roles are prepared, the screen shows the title, then a ring with one piece per role being prepared. A piece fills in the coach blue when its role is done and turns red when it failed; a working piece breathes softly. The middle reads "2/5" over "roles ready". Under the ring, one handwritten line names a role still working, every 1.6 seconds the next one: "Placing 3 answers in Ecommerce Manager · Acme" (1 answer: "Placing 1 answer in …"; a role with no answers this round, only weak lines: "Working on …"). Finished roles drop in under it as chips: "✓ name", or "name: not placed" for a failed one. The ring shows for one role too. The three dots, "All roles at once." and the old list go. Motion stops under reduced motion (the focus screen's rule already does this). Romanian copy included.

A role counts as failed when its own prepare, its answer placing or its check failed (not a 409, which still applies its changes). The end screen's "Some changes could not be prepared" is unchanged.

**Acceptance tests.**
- `prep-ring.test.tsx`: the ring's label and middle count the finished roles ("1 of 3 roles ready", "1/3"); one piece per role with its state; the line names the first working role with its answer count (3, 1 and 0 answers each get their wording); after 1.6 s it names the next working role; done and failed roles show as chips; Romanian wording for 20 answers uses "de".
- `enrich-path.test.tsx`: while one role is still being placed and the other's check failed, the screen shows "1 of 2 roles ready" and the failed role's "not placed" chip.
