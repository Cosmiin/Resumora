# CV journeys

1. Sign up or sign in; confirm email or recover access when needed.
2. Open My Profile. Start manually or upload a supported CV. Import processing and review are recoverable from the account.
3. Review extracted content and uncertain fields, then edit the Base CV. There is one Base CV per account; another upload requires deleting the current Base CV first.
4. Enrich an experience through contextual questions and proposed wording. Confirm answers and explicitly accept the wording to include in the CV.
5. Click Improve skills to extract and clean up skills. All Profile editing pauses during the attempt. Success saves the complete list. At 90 seconds, the attempt is discarded and editing resumes with the previous list; another click starts fresh.
6. Review the CV's language and presentation. Inspect saved history, restore a prior version as a new version, or explicitly remove the Base CV and its associated content. Account authentication remains available.
