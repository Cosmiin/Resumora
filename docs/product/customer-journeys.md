# CV journeys

1. Sign up or sign in; use email confirmation, recovery and password-change flows as needed.
2. Open My Profile. Start manually or upload a supported CV. Background processing can continue while the user navigates; unfinished review is recoverable from the account.
3. Review extracted facts and uncertain fields, then confirm the intended content. Later imports can add selected information without replacing unrelated edits.
4. Enrich an experience through saved questions, context, scores and suggested wording. Accept changes explicitly. Review skills across experiences and keep or remove suggestions.
5. Choose document language, create and review translations, adjust photo/template presentation, preview and export a PDF.
6. Inspect history, restore an earlier version as a new saved version, or explicitly remove the Base CV and its associated content. Account authentication remains available.
