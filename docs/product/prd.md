# Resumora product

My Profile is the main workspace. A person can sign in, import a PDF or Word CV, review extracted facts, edit manually, and enrich their experience through questions and explicitly accepted wording. Skills, source evidence and uncertainty remain visible to the person making the decision.

The product retains saved CV history and restoration, additive imports, language versions, photo controls, document templates, preview and PDF export. Authentication and account controls remain separate from CV content. Removing the Base CV clears its content, history, language copies and associated private files while preserving the account.

Interface language and document language are independent. Extraction preserves source wording; changes that add or rewrite content require explicit review. Failed or uncertain saves must retain the user's draft and offer recovery without silently rerunning paid generation.
