## Evidence limitations

No provider account, negotiated quote, deployment, load test, actual token trace or employer ATS integration was accessed. Pricing pages contain multiple service modes; only rows explicitly named above feed the calculator. Public alias availability does not establish account entitlement or stable model behavior. Snapshot/returned-model IDs must be pinned and recorded at implementation. React-PDF official page fetch failed; it remains an unevaluated rendering alternative rather than a verified primary recommendation. No previous CVTailor repository or screenshot assets were accessed.
