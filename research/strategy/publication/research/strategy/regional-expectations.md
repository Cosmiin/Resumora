# Romanian/EU expectations — evidence and recommendations v1

Access date: 2026-10-23. This document separates official evidence from proposed product behavior. No survey of Romanian job seekers, nationwide ATS adoption study or willingness-to-pay experiment was conducted.

## Verified public context

- Europass is a free EU profile/CV alternative, supports multiple CV versions and 31 document languages: [Europass](https://europass.europa.eu/en/create-europass-cv), [EURES](https://eures.europa.eu/jobseekers/europass_en). A persistent profile or multilingual CV alone is not unique positioning.
- eJobs terms describe two CV versions, Romanian and English, and visibility choices: [eJobs §4](https://www.ejobs.ro/termeni). Romanian and English support is a credible local pattern; the source does not establish all users' language needs or willingness to pay.
- Commercial localized alternatives advertise RON plans and Romanian tailoring/building: [CVmaker pricing](https://www.cvmaker.ro/preturi), [CVExpert](https://cvexpert.ro/). See dated exact amounts/caveats in subscriptions.md. No local-market demand/share conclusion follows.
- ESCO supplies multilingual occupation/skill concepts and variants: [Commission](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/about-esco/what-esco), [terms](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/about-esco/escopedia/escopedia/non-preferred-term). Use RO/EN concept mappings carefully; no taxonomy proves language proficiency or credential eligibility.
- Official EU guidance makes professional recognition country/profession-dependent: [regulated professions](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/work/professional-qualifications/regulated-professions/index_en.htm). Translating a qualification name cannot establish authority to practise. Our product should refer users to competent authorities rather than decide legal equivalence.
- Photo advice varies even in official/service guidance: Europass suggests a professional photo; CVmaker.ro's FAQ recommends omitting it for Romanian applications. Neither is a universal employer/legal mandate. Do not force a photograph or infer national preference from these two sources.
- Commission guidance emphasizes purpose/minimization, accuracy, retention, security and clear information about recipients/transfers/rights: [GDPR principles](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/principles-gdpr_en). These are design inputs; architecture/professional review must determine legal bases, contracts and processing details.
- EU commerce guidance addresses total price, contract duration/termination and payment acknowledgement, with specific withdrawal rules: [Your Europe](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm), [Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/2022-05-28). Do not substitute a competitor's refund window for our legal obligations. No current Romanian tax rate or tax classification is selected here.

## Recommended behavior — baseline-aligned, choices unapproved

Use Romanian plain language with independently chosen English/RO CV output; retain diacritics and original evidence when translating. Prefer localized Month YYYY labels while preserving year-only precision. Explain Base CV, job variant, user confirmation and AI suggestion with concrete examples. Provide structured forms as an alternative to chat; support mobile and keyboard workflows, save/retry and explicit empty/error states. Accessibility/readability are product quality requirements, not established conversion results.

Full residential address, photo, age/date of birth, marital status and unrelated sensitive details should not be mandatory for this CV creation task. Baseline explicitly makes full address optional. Ask for city/country and professional contact/link fields as useful, keep export visibility controllable and do not use protected/personal traits in scoring. This is a minimization recommendation, not a claim that every employer forbids those fields.

Expose professional authorization and language requirements as job-specific evidence gaps. Preserve exact original qualification alongside any reviewed translation; request confirmation without manufacturing equivalence. A required English level needs supported evidence/confirmation, not an English-looking document alone.

Publish clear retention/deletion/portability and AI processing choices after architecture review. EU hosting alone does not demonstrate compliant subprocessors or transfers. Private-by-default career data and explicit sharing are recommended; do not copy a job platform's visibility default without deliberate approval.

For the 1/3/6-month concept, show complete period amount, currency/tax treatment once approved, renewal amount/date, limits, cancellation path/effect and failed-payment recovery. Our selling prices, refunds, free access and post-cancellation document rights remain D-05/07/08. Specialist review should resolve consumer/service/digital-content and tax details before any real payment.
