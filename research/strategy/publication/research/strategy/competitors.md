## Interpretation — recommendations and hypotheses

- **Established baseline:** repeated use of profiles, versions, AI bullets, templates and job keywords in this sample makes any one of them a weak uniqueness claim. This is an inference from the cited rows, not a market-wide conclusion.
- **Hypothesis H-01:** the useful differentiation is a coherent trust workflow: a confirmed fact bank, visible evidence for each requirement, narrow bilingual equivalents, proposal review and a text-extraction-tested export. Emerging claims above mean even “truthful AI tailoring” alone is not demonstrably unique.
- **Hypothesis H-02:** Romanian clarity and low cognitive load may matter more than advanced job tracking. No customer interviews or willingness-to-pay evidence were collected. Do not claim Romanian users lack alternatives.
- **Learning from competitors:** Teal's short guided questions show that enrichment need not be a blank chat; Europass demonstrates durable profile-to-CV selection; Jobscan separates some quality coaching from matching. Our proposed implementation should explicitly store confirmations and evidence rather than merely instruct users to check generated text.

## Extension boundary

Verified examples cover capture/tracking (Teal/Jobscan/Enhancv) and a broader import/autofill workflow (Rezi). Listings are not permission audits or proof every job board works. The initial extension deferral recommendation was superseded by owner feedback. Current R-A recommends focused consented capture with paste/manual recovery, while launch breadth remains D-01. Capture only after user action, show extracted text/source and let users correct it. Automatic applications and bulk LinkedIn scraping are outside the proposed MVP. Architecture owns permission, authentication, site-policy and store-review details.

## What this research cannot establish

No account tests, sample exports, parser benchmarks, support/refund experiences, causal hiring outcomes or cost-per-acquisition data. Lack of public documentation does not prove lack of a competitor capability. Our roadmap must compete on tested task success, not a claimed exclusive feature list.
