## Design implications — recommendations, not approved terms

The sample includes free document creation, feature/AI gating, trials converting to recurring subscriptions, long-period discounts, lifetime access and expiring credits. It does **not** establish willingness to pay for our product or the typical duration of a Romanian job search.

For the baseline's **1/3/6-month concept**, show full amount due, recurring interval, next renewal date/amount, included operation limits and cancellation effect on existing documents. Keep price fields marked “not approved” in discovery. Make cancellation discoverable and confirm it. State whether original/edit/export access survives cancellation; that remains D-07 rather than copying a competitor restriction. Avoid a cheap-download label that conceals a recurring term.

Meter costly operations rather than price every manual correction or preview. Candidate policy for review: correcting an extraction error and retrying our failed operation should not consume a new allowance. Commercial limits, rollover, free-tier economics and prices require product/budget approval with architecture's measured usage model. Competitor “unlimited” claims do not justify an unlimited cost promise.

Consumer disclosure/withdrawal/tax classification require Romanian/EU professional review before selling: [Your Europe](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm), [Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/2022-05-28). This research does not decide whether this service follows a particular digital-content withdrawal exception.
