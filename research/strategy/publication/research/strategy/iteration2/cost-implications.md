## Free-first pilot is a conditional operating goal

Snapshot limits: Supabase 500 MB database/1 GB files/5 GB egress, two projects, pause after seven inactive days; Browser Run 600 seconds/day and three concurrent sessions; Worker Free 10 ms CPU/128 MB. Official URLs and verification date are retained in the private free-pilot output and [portable evidence summary](internal-evidence.md); no independent recheck here.

The model assumes **50 ms/request**, so fully-free runtime is unproven even for the smallest modeled cohort. At 100 users × 10 MB, files fill 1 GB without headroom. 100 users average 210 browser seconds/day, but putting monthly exports on one day requires 6,300 seconds, beyond the daily allowance. Quotas do not carry over. At 25 users, modeled AI + separate backup is **USD 0.92694862**, conditional and explicitly **not complete service cost**; render burst and CPU assumptions still fail. OpenAI inference is paid.

Implication: prototype understandable queue/limit/retry/manual recovery, keep edits safe, meter operations and require owner-approved budget/upgrade policy. No automatic paid upgrade, no promise of zero cost/unlimited use or a proven 25/100-user capacity. Product copy should say what the user can do next, not expose Worker CPU settings.
