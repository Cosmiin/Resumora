## Questions and complexity

Seven primary decisions are placed at their relevant journey step: initial validation group; progressive questions versus blocking interview; matching hierarchy; application creation/depth; capture boundary; access-prompt timing; quota recovery. Seven progressive cards cover remaining D-IDs. Every card has alternatives, consequences, recommendation, scope/cost implications and open status. Fixed requested features are not asked again. [Review blueprint](iteration2/decision-review-blueprint.md), [machine-readable cards](iteration2/decision-interview.v2.json), [D-01–14 register](../../docs/product/decisions.md).

Propose plain application records rather than a full CRM; supplied own-profile text rather than account integration; deterministic immediate diagnosis plus cached questions for the selected section; selective accepted changes rather than full regeneration. These are simplification hypotheses, not approved cuts. Capture, LinkedIn, applications and readiness remain in discovery; launch depth is explicit. [Prioritized backlog](../../docs/product/implementation-backlog.md).

No autonomous applications/messages, broad scraping, purchased contacts, employer-score simulation, hiring guarantees, native apps or default vector/microservice infrastructure. OCR, DOCX export, output breadth, reminders and deeper integrations need explicit depth decisions or measured evidence. Do not silently assign requested capture or application features to a later release.
