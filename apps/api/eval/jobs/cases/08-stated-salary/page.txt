Copper Fern Manufacturing — Careers
Maintenance Electrician
Sheffield, United Kingdom | On site | Permanent, full-time
Salary: GBP 38,000–44,000 per year, depending on experience.

Copper Fern Manufacturing produces reusable metal containers. We are recruiting a Maintenance Electrician to keep our production equipment running safely. This role works a fixed daytime shift at our Sheffield factory.

Responsibilities
- Carry out planned electrical maintenance.
- Diagnose electrical faults on production equipment.
- Keep maintenance records up to date.

Essential requirements
- A Level 3 electrical installation qualification.
- Three years of industrial electrical maintenance experience.
- Knowledge of the current wiring regulations.
- Experience following electrical isolation procedures.

Desirable, not essential
- Experience troubleshooting programmable logic controllers.

We offer employer pension contributions, paid leave and supplied workwear. Overtime is voluntary and paid separately. Interviews include a supervised practical discussion of maintenance tasks. Apply through our careers form before 30 November 2026.
