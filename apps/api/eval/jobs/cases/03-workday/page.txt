Financial Reporting Analyst
Apply
Locations: Edinburgh
Time type: Full time

Job requisition ID: FIN-2048

Northglass Energy develops monitoring equipment for renewable power sites. The finance team needs a Financial Reporting Analyst to support monthly reporting. This permanent role is based in our Edinburgh office, with hybrid working available.

Responsibilities
- Prepare monthly financial reporting packs.
- Investigate differences between forecasts and actual spending.
- Support the annual external audit.

Essential qualifications
- A degree in accounting.
- Two years of financial reporting experience.
- Working knowledge of IFRS.
- Proficiency with Microsoft Excel.
- Experience reconciling general ledger accounts.

Desirable qualifications
- Experience using Workday Financial Management.

Benefits: pension contributions, paid study leave and an employee assistance programme. We provide reasonable adjustments throughout recruitment.
