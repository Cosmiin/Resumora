Bristol BS1 | Full-time

Full job description
Cedar Basket supplies reusable packaging to independent retailers. We need a Customer Support Specialist to answer questions from our business customers. The role is office based, Monday to Friday, with no weekend shifts.

Duties
- Answer incoming customer emails.
- Explain delivery updates to customers.
- Record recurring product issues for the operations team.

Requirements
- One year of customer support experience.
- Clear written English.
- Experience using a support ticket system.
- Ability to use spreadsheets for order tracking.

Preferred
- Experience using Zendesk.

Benefits include paid annual leave, staff discounts and a cycle to work scheme. Full training on our packaging range is provided. Salary is discussed at interview and is not listed here.
