Warehouse Operative
Liverpool, United Kingdom | On site | Full-time, temporary
Six-month appointment, Monday to Friday, fixed early shift

Amber Quay Logistics handles stock for small homeware retailers. We are hiring a Warehouse Operative for our Liverpool distribution centre during a stockroom expansion. You will work in a team with a supervisor available on every shift.

Main duties
- Pick customer orders from marked storage bays.
- Pack goods using the supplied instructions.
- Record completed orders in the stock system.

Required
- Six months of warehouse experience.
- Experience using a handheld barcode scanner.
- Ability to read written safety instructions in English.
- Experience checking goods against a delivery note.

All equipment training is provided during paid induction. There are no preferred qualifications for this vacancy. Benefits include supplied workwear, paid breaks and an employee discount programme. Pay information is not included in this posting.
