Senior Backend Engineer
Lumen Orchard | London, United Kingdom | Hybrid | Full time

Lumen Orchard builds inventory software for independent food shops. We are adding a senior engineer to the team that owns our ordering API. This is a permanent role with two days each week in our London office.

What you will do
- Build services that keep shop inventory up to date.
- Review changes with the engineering team.
- Help investigate production incidents during working hours.

Required qualifications
- At least five years of backend engineering experience.
- Experience building services with TypeScript.
- Experience using PostgreSQL.
- Experience designing HTTP APIs.
- Written English suitable for technical documentation.

Nice to have
- Experience operating services on Cloudflare Workers.

Benefits include 28 days of annual leave, a learning budget and a pension contribution. Compensation will be discussed during the interview process; no salary range is published.
