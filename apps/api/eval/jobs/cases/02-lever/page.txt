Pebble Atlas makes planning tools for community organisations. Our small design team is looking for a product designer to help volunteers coordinate their work. You will report to the design manager and work with a dedicated product team.

The work
- Turn research findings into usable interface designs.
- Prototype new scheduling flows.
- Check released features with customers.

What you need
- Three years of product design experience.
- Proficiency with Figma.
- Experience running usability interviews.
- A portfolio showing shipped digital products.

Preferred, but not required
- Experience designing accessible interfaces.
- Experience maintaining a design system.

We offer flexible working hours, paid leave and a home office allowance. This position is remote within Ireland. The application process includes a portfolio conversation and a paid design exercise.
