Vacancies / Primary School Teacher

Responsibilities
- Plan lessons aligned with the school curriculum.

Essential criteria
- Qualified Teacher Status in England.
- Two years of primary classroom teaching experience.
- Knowledge of safeguarding procedures in schools.
- Experience adapting lessons for pupils with additional learning needs.

Desirable

Benefits include a pension contribution and access to professional development sessions. The school provides teaching materials and classroom support. Salary details are available during the application process and are not stated here.
