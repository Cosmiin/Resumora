Juniper Civic Studio
Project Coordinator
Leeds, United Kingdom | Hybrid | Full-time, permanent

We help community groups plan improvements to shared spaces. Juniper Civic Studio is adding a Project Coordinator to organise project information across several small teams. You will work with a project manager and join a weekly planning meeting.

What you will do
- Maintain project schedules.
- Share progress updates with partner organisations.
- Prepare agendas for planning meetings.

Must have
- Two years of project coordination experience.
- Experience maintaining project schedules.
- Ability to write clear meeting notes.
- Experience using spreadsheets to track budgets.

Nice to have — these are optional, and applications without them are welcome
- Experience using Asana.
- A PRINCE2 Foundation certification.
- Experience working with community organisations.

Benefits include flexible hours, a learning allowance and paid volunteering days. We do not list a salary range. The interview will focus on how you organise project information, rather than on formal presentations.
