Cloudberry Pressworks
Content Marketing Specialist
Remote within Canada | Full-time, permanent

Cloudberry Pressworks creates publishing tools for independent magazines. We are looking for a Content Marketing Specialist to help our customers discover practical publishing advice. You will join a small marketing team that works across Canadian time zones.

Responsibilities
- Plan a monthly editorial calendar.
- Write product education articles.
- Review campaign performance with the marketing lead.

Required qualifications
- Three years of content marketing experience.
- A portfolio of published English-language articles.
- Experience researching search keywords.
- Experience using Google Analytics.
- Experience publishing articles in a content management system.

Nice to have
- Experience creating email newsletters.
- Experience working in magazine publishing.

We offer flexible hours, a home office allowance and paid professional development. The role has no travel requirement. We have not published a salary range. Apply with your resume and links to existing writing samples.
