Bicycle Mechanic
Mosswheel Cycles — York, United Kingdom
Part-time, three days a week, on site

Our independent bicycle workshop repairs commuter and cargo bikes. We have space for a Bicycle Mechanic to join our small workshop team. This is a permanent part-time position with a regular daytime rota.

You will carry out scheduled services, diagnose mechanical problems and explain completed repairs to customers. The workshop manager handles bookings and ordering, so you can focus on safe, careful repair work.

To do this job you need
- Two years of professional bicycle repair experience.
- Experience servicing hydraulic brakes.
- Experience adjusting derailleur gears.
- Ability to document repairs in a workshop log.

We provide workshop tools, an annual tool care allowance and staff pricing on parts. There are no additional preferred qualifications. Send an application through the workshop form with a short description of your repair experience.
