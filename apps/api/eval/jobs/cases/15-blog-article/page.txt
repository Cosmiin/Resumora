How small teams write clearer job descriptions
Published 2 November 2026 | Editorial team

A clear job description helps readers understand what a role involves. Start with a specific title and explain the work in everyday language. Separate essential qualifications from optional experience, so applicants can decide whether to apply.

For example, an imaginary company might advertise a Data Engineer who knows SQL. Another example might describe a part-time workshop assistant. These are teaching examples, not open positions. This article is not hiring for either role.

When discussing salary, explain the currency and pay period. Avoid copying an unrelated salary range from another organisation. Keep application instructions near the end of the posting, and remove unnecessary page clutter.

Before publishing, ask a colleague to check that each requirement has a purpose. This article offers general writing advice and has no hiring employer, application form or open vacancy.
