A calmer, more compact skills section

Three visual proposals using your current CV style. Switch between them or compare all three below.

These are visual concepts. Editing, disclosure and side-panel interactions will be implemented after you choose.

To select a proposal, reply 1, 2 or 3 in the chat. You can also request changes or combine ideas.

1 · Editorial list

Keep every skill visible in two compact text groups. Move add, rename and remove controls behind Edit. Best for scanning the complete inventory.
